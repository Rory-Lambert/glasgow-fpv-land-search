# Shortlist: candidate sites for the club

*Generated from the Scottish Vacant and Derelict Land Survey. Filtered to
council-owned land in the Glasgow area, football-pitch sized, then scored for
suitability as an FPV racing course. See [README.md](README.md) for the method
and [outputs/all_scored_sites.csv](outputs/all_scored_sites.csv) for the full data.*

## The headline

Once you filter to open, council-owned, pitch-sized land and screen out the
airport flight-restriction zones, a clear pattern emerges: **former sports
pitches and recreation grounds** rise to the top. They're already flat, drained,
open, recreationally zoned, and free of neighbouring tenements — almost a
like-for-like with the Craufurdland plot we run now.

**Top recommendation: the Former Blaes Pitch at Cambuslang.** It's an old ash
football pitch — exactly the right shape and size — on open recreational land the
council has no firm plans for, well inside Glasgow and clear of both airport zones.

## Tier 1 — former pitches & recreation grounds (best fit)

### 1. Former Blaes Pitch, Westburn Road, Cambuslang ⭐
- **Council:** South Lanarkshire · **Size:** 1.25 ha · **Score:** +11
- **Why:** A former ash football pitch — literally pitch-shaped and sized. 7.7 km SE of the city centre, 18 km from the nearest airport. Former "Recreation & Leisure" use with no firm development timescale.
- **Watch for:** Confirm it hasn't been re-earmarked; check road access and any residential frontage on Westburn Road.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.820998,-4.151503 · *(site code 8445387)*

### 2. Former Recreation Ground, MacArthur Avenue, Glenmavis
- **Council:** North Lanarkshire · **Size:** 1.63 ha · **Score:** +11
- **Why:** Open former recreation ground on the semi-rural edge of Glenmavis village — good sightlines, room to grow.
- **Watch for:** 7.3 km from **Cumbernauld Airport** — outside the 5 km FRZ but the closest of our picks to a general-aviation field, so check airspace carefully.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.883586,-4.002923 · *(site code NL008491903)*

### 3. Wheatholme Park North, Wheatholm Street, Rawyards, Airdrie
- **Council:** North Lanarkshire · **Size:** 0.73 ha · **Score:** +11
- **Why:** Bang-on football-pitch size, former recreation ground.
- **Watch for:** Smallest of the tier-1 sites — fine for a course like ours but limited room to expand. ~8 km from Cumbernauld Airport.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.874173,-3.970852 · *(site code NL008491906)*

## Tier 2 — open former-amenity / agricultural land

### 4. East of Lochgoin Avenue, Darnley / Nitshill
- **Council:** Glasgow City · **Size:** 1.07 ha · **Score:** +11
- **Why:** Former "Passive Open Space" on the SW edge of the city, 9.9 km out. Open ground, within Glasgow itself.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.917933,-4.372224

### 5. Rear of 42–78 Gardenside Crescent (Cambuslang area)
- **Council:** Glasgow City · **Size:** 0.78 ha · **Score:** +10
- **Why:** Former agricultural land, open, 6.7 km from the centre — close to the Cambuslang blaes pitch, so a two-birds site visit.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.827945,-4.161786

### 6. Northern Site, Lomond Industrial Estate, Alexandria
- **Council:** West Dunbartonshire · **Size:** 1.98 ha · **Score:** +10
- **Why:** The largest of the strong candidates — former agricultural land with real room for a bigger course.
- **Watch for:** 25 km from the centre — a stretch for members travelling from Glasgow.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.996137,-4.574884

## Staying in East Ayrshire (our home council)

If we'd rather keep it on home turf — likely the easiest relationship to build on,
given we're already based there — the open-recreation options are thinner. The best
council-owned, pitch-sized candidate is:

- **Former Residential Development, Stoner Crescent, Auchinleck** — 1.56 ha, vacant land, no firm development plan. https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.474799,-4.292637

It's a cleared housing site rather than open recreation land, so a site visit
matters more here.

## What we deliberately left out

- **Cleared housing plots hemmed in by tenements** (e.g. Firpark Street, Wellfield Street in Glasgow City). They score well on paper but are poor for FPV: no separation from uninvolved people, blocked sightlines, and high noise-objection risk. They sit below the open sites in the ranking.
- **The Paisley / Ferguslie sites** (Drums Avenue, Bankfoot Road, Blackstoun Road). All within ~2 km of Glasgow Airport — **inside the flight-restriction zone**. Excluded from the ranked table entirely.
