# Shortlist: candidate sites for the club

*Generated from the Scottish Vacant and Derelict Land Survey. Filtered to
council-owned land in the Glasgow area, football-pitch sized, then scored for
suitability as an FPV racing course **and for how close it is to our members**.
See [README.md](README.md) for the method and
[outputs/all_scored_sites.csv](outputs/all_scored_sites.csv) for the full data.*

> **Scoring now includes member travel.** Alongside site quality (type,
> development pressure, former use, size), each site earns up to +4 for being
> close to members' postcodes — currently **4 members (G31, G73, G20, and FK15 /
> Dunblane)**. The Dunblane member sits well north of the others, so it widens
> the spread and lifts the northern sites. Add yours to
> [data/members.md](data/members.md) and the ranking rebalances. Distances below
> are straight-line means; per-member driving times are on each site's issue.

## The headline

Once you filter to open, council-owned, pitch-sized land and screen out the
airport flight-restriction zones, a clear pattern emerges: **former sports
pitches and recreation grounds** rise to the top. They're already flat, drained,
open, recreationally zoned, and free of neighbouring tenements — almost a
like-for-like with the Craufurdland plot we run now.

**Top recommendation: the Former Blaes Pitch at Cambuslang.** It's an old ash
football pitch — exactly the right shape and size — on open recreational land the
council has no firm plans for, well inside Glasgow and clear of both airport zones.

## Tier 1 — former pitches & recreation grounds (best fit)

### 1. Former Blaes Pitch, Westburn Road, Cambuslang ⭐
- **Council:** South Lanarkshire · **Size:** 1.25 ha · **Score:** +13 · **Avg member distance:** 16.0 km
- **Why:** A former ash football pitch — literally pitch-shaped and sized. Former "Recreation & Leisure" use with no firm development timescale, clear of both airport zones. Joint-top on score and the strongest all-rounder: high site quality and central to the members.
- **Watch for:** **Housing on the SW side** — the proximity screen puts the nearest homes ~11 m from the (approximate) edge, inside a 50 m separation line. This may constrain the usable flight line or need permissions; check it early. Also confirm it hasn't been re-earmarked, and check road access. See the housing screen on [issue #1](../../issues/1).
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.820998,-4.151503 · *(site code 8445387)*

### 2. Former Recreation Ground, MacArthur Avenue, Glenmavis
- **Council:** North Lanarkshire · **Size:** 1.63 ha · **Score:** +13 · **Avg member distance:** 20.2 km
- **Why:** Open former recreation ground on the semi-rural edge of Glenmavis village — good sightlines, room to grow. Joint-top on score, and its northerly position suits the Dunblane end of the membership.
- **Watch for:** 7.3 km from **Cumbernauld Airport** — outside the 5 km FRZ but the closest of our picks to a general-aviation field, so check airspace carefully.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.883586,-4.002923 · *(site code NL008491903)*

### 3. Wheatholme Park North, Wheatholm Street, Rawyards, Airdrie
- **Council:** North Lanarkshire · **Size:** 0.73 ha · **Score:** +13 · **Avg member distance:** 21.8 km
- **Why:** Bang-on football-pitch size, former recreation ground. Joint-top on score.
- **Watch for:** Smallest of the tier-1 sites — fine for a course like ours but limited room to expand. ~8 km from Cumbernauld Airport.
- **Map:** https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.874173,-3.970852 · *(site code NL008491906)*

## Staying in East Ayrshire (our home council)

If we'd rather keep it on home turf — likely the easiest relationship to build on,
given we're already based there — the open-recreation options are thinner. The best
council-owned, pitch-sized candidate is:

- **Former Residential Development, Stoner Crescent, Auchinleck** — 1.56 ha, vacant land, no firm development plan, **score +9**. https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=55.474799,-4.292637

It's a cleared housing site rather than open recreation land, so a site visit
matters more here. At **~53 km average from the members** it's the furthest of the
shortlist and sits well down the ranking on travel — worth it only if staying
in-council outweighs the extra distance for people.

## What we deliberately left out

- **Cleared housing plots hemmed in by tenements** (e.g. Firpark Street, Wellfield Street in Glasgow City). Being central they now score highly on both openness and *travel* (they're close to members), so they climb the raw ranking — but they remain poor for FPV: no separation from uninvolved people, blocked sightlines, and high noise-objection risk. We keep them off the shortlist on suitability grounds despite the score.
- **The Paisley / Ferguslie sites** (Drums Avenue, Bankfoot Road, Blackstoun Road). All within ~2 km of Glasgow Airport — **inside the flight-restriction zone**. Excluded from the ranked table entirely.
