# Council contacts — Community Asset Transfer

In Scotland the route for a charity/community body to take on council-owned land
is **Community Asset Transfer (CAT)** under Part 5 of the Community Empowerment
(Scotland) Act 2015 — a statutory *right to request* to lease, manage or buy
council land. Each site issue links to the relevant council's CAT page below.

First step for any council is usually an **Expression of Interest / enquiry form**
on these pages, or an email to the contact address.

Edit this table to correct a link or add a contact. `analysis/issue_body.py`
reads it to fill in each issue; match the council name exactly to the
`local_authority` values in the survey.
